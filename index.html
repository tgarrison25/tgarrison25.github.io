Research

My guiding principle for research is simple rules → complex behavior. I've researched tilings, consecutive numbers, combinatorial games, integer partitions, sunflowers, and more. I like to pose my own problems and get my hands dirty with computation — SAT solving and brute-forcing small examples are my favorite ways to build intuition.

Packing and dissection

Rectangle packing and square dissection — questions where the extremal object is small enough to search for but large enough that the search has to be clever. PackIt! is a game built from one such problem; you can play it here.

Automated reasoning

Encoding combinatorial statements into SAT, breaking symmetry well enough that the solver finishes, and producing proofs a referee can check without trusting the solver.

Sequences and divisibility

Permutations constrained by divisibility conditions, where the counting function turns on the primality of nearby integers — see weakly consecutive sequences.
