Undergraduate research advising

Advisor · University of Florida · ongoing

We study the 1 × n restriction of PackIt!, in which pieces must be placed in a single row: on turn t a piece of size t goes into a gap, splitting it into two smaller gaps, and gaps smaller than the current piece size are dead. The project is a computational study of which player wins from a given position.

Joint work with three undergraduates.
