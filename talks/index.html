Solving PackIt!

57th Southeastern International Conference on Combinatorics, Graph Theory and Computing (SEICCGTC) · March 2026

Abstract

PackIt! is a 2-player game played on an n × n board where on the ith turn you play a rectangle of area i or i+1. As in normal play, you win if you move last. The game strategy is surprisingly complex and is unsolved for n ≥ 11. We conjecture that for games of odd board size, Player 1 wins, and for even board size, Player 2 wins. This conjecture has been verified for n < 11. We talk about the computation of small cases and the things we know about the strategy.
