Two axes: collaboration (distinct Erdos-1 coauthors) and problem-solving (importance-weighted Erdos problems). Alpha trades one against the other and is literally each axis's share of the score: at α = 0.5, collaboration can contribute at most 50 points and problem-solving at most 50. Each axis is divided by its leading human — Alon's 60 coauthors, Szemeredi's 72.2 problem points — so one of Alon's coauthors is worth exactly 50/60. Nobody scores 100, because no real person leads both axes; Alon tops the list at 77.4. Data: 14703 people with Erdos number ≤ 2 or problem credit.

Weighting

Where would you land?

A median credited problem is worth 1.9 points; a tier-5 problem solved outright is worth 5. Partial progress counts half. So "two decent problems" ≈ 4 points.

Percentile is mid-rank: ties are split, not counted in your favour. It matters here — 10,612 people have exactly one Erdos-1 coauthor and nothing else, so they all score the same and the tie block is 72% of the population.
